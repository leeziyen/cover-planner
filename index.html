A quick word on CPF

  • OA (Ordinary Account): housing, approved insurance, investment, education. 2.5% p.a.
  • SA (Special Account, closes at 55): retirement savings and approved investments. 4% p.a.
  • MA (MediSave Account): hospital bills and approved medical insurance (MediShield Life, Integrated Shield Plans). 4% p.a., capped at the Basic Healthcare Sum (S$79,000 in 2026).
  • RA (Retirement Account, from 55): formed from OA + SA up to the Full Retirement Sum; funds CPF LIFE for monthly payouts for life.
  • DPS (Dependants' Protection Scheme): free opt-out term life, automatically covers CPF members 21–65, up to S$70,000 (S$55,000 from 60). Administered by Great Eastern — a safety net, not a full life plan.

Plan types at a glance, for the client

Term: the lowest premium for the highest cover over a fixed period. It ends when the term ends and builds no savings.

Whole life, limited pay: cover for life while premiums stop after a set number of years, and the policy builds cash value.

Whole life, investment-linked: lifelong cover with flexible top-ups and withdrawals. Values move with the funds chosen and are not guaranteed.

Wealth accumulation: built to grow savings for a goal or legacy, with basic protection attached. Values move with the market.
